# Privacy Policy

## Who we are
The Plant Breeders and Innovators Network (PIN) System is managed by the Crop Biotechnology Center (CBC) to accelerate collaboration, transparency, and innovation across the Philippine crop biotechnology ecosystem. This policy explains how CBC collects, stores, protects, and uses personal data from individuals who interact with the PIN System.

## Personal data we collect
We collect only the data that is required to deliver features and comply with legal obligations, including:
- **Identity and authentication data:** name, username, email, role, institution, profile picture, and password hashes when you create an account.
- **Operational data:** login timestamps, IP addresses, device information, session metadata, audit logs, and actions taken within the PIN modules.
- **Communications:** messages, support requests, feedback, and any files you upload when interacting with support or research workflows.
- **PMIS/registry data:** information shared with CBC for registry, mapping, or reporting purposes such as breeder credentials, crop trial summaries, or partnership documentation.

## How we use your data
CBC processes personal data to:
- authenticate and authorize access to PIN workflows (PbMap, TWG data, research dashboards, etc.).
- power the searchable breeder map, filter pipeline, and reporting visualizations while keeping personal identifiers confined to authorized roles.
- keep you informed about system updates, new features, training events, and policy changes.
- fulfill legal obligations, including audit, compliance, and requests from regulatory bodies when required by law.
- protect the PIN System by monitoring for abuse, suspicious activity, and security threats.

## Legal basis and retention
Processing is based on the performance of a contract (system access), compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules, and CBC's legitimate interest in securing the network. We retain data only for as long as needed to support PIN services or as required by law, then securely archive or dispose of it.

## Data sharing and transfers
We never sell personal data. CBC may share information with:
- trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, identity, and notification partners) that are contractually bound by confidentiality and data protection standards.
- government agencies or regulators when mandated by law or legitimate legal process.
- collaborating research partners only when you explicitly authorize it or when data is submitted within a shared research workflow.

Cross-border transfers (if they occur) rely on safeguards such as encryption and data processing agreements.

## Security
We protect your data with encryption at rest and in transit, least-privilege access controls, periodic audits, and proactive monitoring. Access to sensitive data is limited to staff and partners who need it to deliver PIN services. Physical, administrative, and technical controls align with CBC's information security policies.

## Your rights
Under the Data Privacy Act, you may:
- request access to the data we hold about you.
- ask for corrections or updates to inaccurate or incomplete information.
- object to processing that is not required by law.
- request blocking, erasure, or destruction of data that is no longer necessary or when processing is unlawful.
- withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.
- lodge a complaint with CBC or the National Privacy Commission.

Requests may require verification to ensure we respond only to the rightful account holder and may be limited by legal exceptions.

## Cookies and tracking
The PIN System uses minimal cookies for session management, security, and preference storage. No marketing cookie is set without your consent. You can revoke browser-based cookies at any time via your browser settings.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this notice when our practices or legal requirements change. Any revision will be published here with the latest revision date.
